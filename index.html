The belief in anito are referred to as Anitism.
Anito are sacrifices, offerings, ceremonies and also a formal act of worship conducted by a Babaylan.
The Anitism religion is devoted to the Gods, ancestors, nature spirits and other entities, collectively known as Diwata among the Visayans.
