Diwata of harvest:

  • Laon: Diwata who dwells in the Kanlaon volcano on Buglas island; Diwata of harvest.

Diwata of skies:

  • Makaptan: Diwata who dwells highest in the sky; sends disease and death among them.

Diwata of death:

  • Sidapa: Diwata who lives in the sacred Mount Madya-as; determines the day of a person's death by marking every newborn's lifespan on a very tall tree on Madya-as.

Diwata of redemption:

  • Pandaki: Diwata who is given ritual offerings so that a soul of the deceased will not be taken by the Gods responsible for torment in the afterlife.

Diwata of torment:

  • Simuran: tormentor Diwata.

  • Siginarugan: tormentor Diwata.

Diwata of war:

  • Balangaw: the rainbow.

  • Inaginid: war Diwata.

  • Makanduk: war Diwata.
